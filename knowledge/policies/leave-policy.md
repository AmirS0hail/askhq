# Alfabolt Leave Policy

This document outlines the official leave policy for all Alfabolt employees. All leave entitlements are per calendar year (January–December) and are non-cumulative unless stated otherwise.

## Types of Leave

### Casual Leave

- **Entitlement:** 3 days per year
- Can be taken without prior approval in case of emergencies
- For planned casual leave, inform your manager at least 1 day in advance
- Cannot be combined with other leave types for more than 3 consecutive days

### Sick Leave

- **Entitlement:** 7 days per year
- Medical certificate required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days
- Inform your manager before start of business day if possible
- Unused sick leave does not carry forward to the next year

### Annual Leave

- **Entitlement:** 14 days per year
- Must be approved by your direct manager at least 1 week in advance
- For leave longer than 5 consecutive days, approval required 2 weeks in advance
- Up to 5 unused annual leave days may carry forward to Q1 of the next year

### Special Leave

#### Birthday Leave

- 1 day per year on your birthday
- If your birthday falls on a weekend or holiday, you may take the nearest working day
- Must be taken within the same calendar month as your birthday

#### Maternity Leave

- 3 months (90 calendar days) of paid leave
- Applicable to all employees who have completed 3 months of continuous employment
- Additional unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be requested
- Return-to-work plan to be discussed with manager before leave begins

#### Paternity Leave

- 1 month (30 calendar days) of paid leave
- Must be taken within 2 months of the child's date of birth or adoption
- Applicable to all employees who have completed 3 months of continuous employment

## Eligibility

- **Casual Leave:** Available from date of joining
- **All other leave types:** Available after 3 months of continuous employment
- Leave entitlements are prorated for employees who join mid-year
- Probationary employees are eligible for casual and sick leave only

## Process

### Before Taking Leave

1. Inform your direct manager at least 1 week in advance (except casual/sick leave)
2. Get written approval via email or the HR portal
3. Inform clients and stakeholders if your absence affects ongoing deliverables
4. Update your Slack status to indicate you are on leave
5. Block your calendar for the leave duration
6. Set up email out-of-office auto-reply with return date and emergency contact

### Recording Leave

- Submit leave request in the HR portal within 48 hours of taking leave
- Retroactive leave requests beyond 7 days require manager and HR approval
- Unapproved absences may be treated as unpaid leave

### Leave During Notice Period

- Annual leave cannot be taken during the notice period without CEO approval
- Sick leave during notice period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration
